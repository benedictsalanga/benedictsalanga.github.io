Orbiting Dot Animation

Explore circular motion through mathematics using sine and cosine.

⌨ Animation Controls

  • SPACE: Pause or resume animation
  • UP ARROW: Increase rotation speed
  • DOWN ARROW: Decrease rotation speed

📐 Mathematics Behind the Animation

The position of the moving dot is calculated using:

  • x = centerX + radius × cos(angle)
  • y = centerY + radius × sin(angle)

Sine and cosine determine the horizontal and vertical positions of the dot as it travels around a circular path.
